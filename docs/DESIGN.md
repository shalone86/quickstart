# Design: three options, one pick

You said you like the Apple Notes design but wanted options workshopped. Here are the three directions I considered, and why I chose the one in the app.

## Option A: "Paper" (Apple Notes, refined)

- Warm off-white paper (`#f7f4ee`), white note "sheets", one golden accent (Apple Notes yellow, a little deeper so it reads on white).
- Sidebar with folders and tags on desktop. Bottom tab bar on phones.
- The note's first line becomes a big bold title automatically, just like Apple Notes.
- Lists are calm text rows grouped by *Today / Yesterday / Previous 7 days / Month*.
- **Pros:** familiar, fast to scan, great for long notes, excellent in dark mode.
- **Cons:** on its own, "make a new note" is a button away, not the first thing you see.

## Option B: "Stream" (Twitter-style capture)

- Home is one big text box ("What's on your mind?") with a feed of notes underneath, newest first, like a timeline.
- Every note is a card, and tags show as chips.
- **Pros:** zero-friction capture. Open the app and type.
- **Cons:** a feed is poor for finding and organizing (folders and long notes feel cramped in cards), and an endless timeline gets noisy fast.

## Option C: "Codex" (monastic manuscript)

- Parchment textures, serif type, red rubrication on headings, drop caps, gold-leaf accents. It fits the name *Scriptorium*.
- **Pros:** beautiful and distinctive, with a nod to your Catholic projects.
- **Cons:** heavier to read on a phone, decoration competes with your writing, and the dark mode is hard to make good.

## The pick: Paper, with Stream's composer and a touch of Codex

The app uses **Option A's structure and calm**, because you already like it and it's the best for actually working with notes. Two things are borrowed from the others:

- **From Stream:** the home screen opens to a **ready-to-type composer** with your **3 most recent notes** below it. That's exactly your first two requests: open to a new note, type like Twitter, press Save, and it's filed.
- **From Codex:** the name, the gold accent, the quill icon, and an optional **Serif (book)** note font in Settings → Appearance.

### Details worth knowing

- **Accent color:** deep gold `#c98a00` in light mode, `#f0b429` in dark mode.
- **Type:** the system font (San Francisco on iPhone) everywhere. Serif and monospaced fonts are available for note text.
- **Touch:** every tap target is at least 34–44px, and the bottom sheets on phones stay above the keyboard.
- **Dark mode** follows your device, or you can set it in Settings.
